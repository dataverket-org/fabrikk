# Products and the catalogue

*Part of [the glossary](README.md).* Each entry says what the word means; the rules behind it are in the page the entry links to.

| Term | Norwegian | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Product** | — | What a provider offers and the catalogue lists, named for customers. It has what it announces, its items and the counts its quota is written in with their defaults, and its terms, published beside it. An organisation takes a product by accepting its terms and gives it back by offboarding. Built from one or more services, see [words beneath the story](system-words.md); not used for the things a product delivers. The control plane knows a product only by its catalogue entry and [the seam](../../method/seam.md). |
| **Example product** | — | The product the stories use, unnamed: an example, not a product. It is only what the control plane can see of any product and is never given behaviour of its own; it has no box on the map, no language and no design. One item, *the example item*; one quota count; the command classes *ask*, *give back* and *read*. Its facts on the board: *Product announced*, *Terms published*, *Terms accepted*, *Resource asked for*, *Resource delivered*, *Resource given back*, *Organisation cleared*. |
| **The control plane** | — | The installation's product, written lower case in prose, which every organisation takes at its creation and holds until it closes. Its listing is a name and terms, with no items, counts or command classes, so it answers the seam trivially and an expiry of its terms refuses no ask. Rules: [the seam](../../method/seam.md) and [Organisation's design](../design/organisation/README.md). |
| **Terms** | Vilkår | A product's terms: what an organisation accepts to take the product, published by the provider's operator into the catalogue beside it as its standing offer; each publication has an identity. New terms carry an effective date, which is the notice, and the old terms expire for an organisation that does not accept again. The terms give the notice every offboarding of the product carries. Rules: [Catalogue's design](../design/catalogue/README.md), [Organisation's commands](../design/organisation/commands.md). |
| **Installation terms** | — | The control plane's terms, accepted by a login once, before it validates or creates an organisation, *Installation terms accepted*, and by the organisation at its creation. A login that only has users in organisations accepts nothing, covered by their agreements. |
| **Effective date** | Ikrafttredelse | The day new terms replace the old for every organisation that has not accepted them. Until it both are listed; pending terms are replaced by a later publication, never withdrawn. |
| **Catalogue** | — | What anyone can read about every provider's products in the installation: what each announces, the counts its quota is written in, its terms current and pending, and whether it is withdrawn. The installation's, filled by the services announcing and the providers publishing terms; a product catalogue in the established sense. |
| **Listing** | — | A product's place in the catalogue, under its provider: Catalogue's one aggregate, in [its design](../design/catalogue/README.md). The installation's, the control plane, is a given of the left edge. |
| **Announcement** | — | What the service that runs a product says about it with *announce*, and again whenever it changes: its name, items with where each is offered and what can be had now, its quota counts with their defaults, and the class of each of its commands. The control plane validates against the counts, defaults, classes and items; the rest is the product's own words. |
| **Catalogue item** | — | A unit a product offers, named in its announcement; a resource is delivered as one item. Shortened to *item*. |
| **Resource** | Ressurs | What a product delivers and an organisation holds, as the control plane names it: the product and the product's own id. A reference, never a box; the industry's word. |
| **Reservation** | Reservasjon | A resource asked for and accepted but not yet delivered, counted against the quota with what is held and released on delivery, failure or cancel. The product's own. |
| **Quota** | Kvote | The provider's ceiling on what an organisation may hold of a product, per count the announcement names, starting from the announcement's default and set by the operator alone, as a cloud's service quota is. On the agreement, read by the product at the ask. |
