# The glossary: the ubiquitous language

The terms of the control plane, for use in conversation, events, documentation and code. Terms are defined in English. Each product has a language of its own; what crosses between a product and the control plane is written in the words here. Established industry terms are reused wherever one fits.

## At a glance

- **Organisation** is the term for the party holding things, everywhere. "Customer" is informal only; "tenant" is system-internal.
- **The agreement** is the organisation's relationship with one provider: the products taken, each with its terms accepted and its quota, and whether it is held, active or ended; it is active once every condition for activation the provider's organisation policy names is met, at once where it names none. Terms are a product's, accepted one product at a time. Every organisation holds one with the installation from its birth, for the control plane, and is never without an agreement.
- **A product is what a provider sells; a service is a unit of software.** The catalogue lists products, each as a listing with its announcement and its terms, the installation's own, the control plane, among them. The stories use the example product, an example and not a product.
- **Words beneath the story** are the system's own: tenant, account, the front desk, instance, admission and Stack's words for the inside of a context. A design page may use no system word that is not there.
- **The model** is the whole: the stories, this glossary, the map, the decisions and the designs, agreed with the domain owner and written in these pages; fabrikk implements it. *The design* is a context's, Organisation's or Tenancy's; *the record* is the audit's; neither names the whole.
