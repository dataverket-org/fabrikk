# Record

*Part of [the glossary](README.md).*

| Term | Norwegian | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Audit record** | — | The record of every command and integration event, each entry the envelope and the outcome and never the payload, append-only, kept for the legally required period. Entries name the actor, a human by the person id where the login is validated and the user id where not, and carry every organisation they concern. An organisation has two records, each a provider's, the installation's one of them. Rules: [the audit decision](../decisions/audit.md) and [Audit](../map/audit.md). |
