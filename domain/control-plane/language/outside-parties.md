# Outside parties

*Part of [the glossary](README.md).*

| Term | Norwegian | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity providers for login** | — | FEIDE, Entra ID, Google and whichever others the installation allows: what a person logs in through. A login proves nothing about who the human is and reveals no external ID; the installation's identity provider takes it, see [words beneath the story](system-words.md). |
| **Means of validation** | — | BankID and Vipps today: what a login validates through, once, by the means the installation's or the organisation's policy names. Each proves a real human and reveals the national identity number, which Person uses only to recognise the same human again. A means that reveals no such number, FEIDE among them, is a login and not validation. |
