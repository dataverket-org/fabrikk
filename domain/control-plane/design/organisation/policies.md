# Policies

*Part of [Organisation's design](README.md).* A policy is Organisation reacting to an event, its own or another context's, with no human asking. The events from other contexts are the imports Organisation declares. The agreement rows hold for every agreement, the installation's among them; where the control plane answers a row differently from a provider's product the row says so.

| Whenever | Organisation does | Lifecycle |
|---|---|---|
| *Login validated* arrives from Person, naming the login and carrying the person id | Marks every user of that login validated and keeps the person id; the owner role becomes grantable to them, and every role of theirs held without applying applies from then on, with no event | Organisation |
| Every condition for activation the provider's policy names is met: checked at the agreement's birth, when a condition is met, and when the provider's policy changes | Activates the agreement, *Agreement activated*. One condition over the provider's list and the agreement, re-evaluated whenever either changes: a provider that names none activates at birth, the installation among them; one that names approval holds the agreement until *approve agreement*, the organisation and the operator told by the agreement entry, which names the conditions unmet; one that drops a condition activates every agreement waiting for it alone. Nothing here goes backwards: a condition named after activation does not deactivate. A held agreement refuses nothing of the owners'; only *may it ask* answers no | Agreement |
| *New terms published* arrives from Catalogue for a product the agreement holds | Marks the product's terms replaced until the effective date, *Terms replaced*; the owner is told. An owner accepts the new terms with *accept terms* at any time after | Agreement |
| *Product withdrawn* arrives from Catalogue for a product the agreement holds | Marks the product withdrawn on its entry, shown in the overview; nothing of the agreement changes | Agreement |
| The effective date of replaced terms passes with no *Terms accepted* naming the new terms | Marks the product's terms expired, *Terms expired*: the product refuses new asks, what is held stays under the old terms, and the provider may request offboarding of the product. Held as the date on the product's entry, so a restart loses nothing. For the control plane an expiry refuses nothing, since it has no command class; it lets the installation's operator request offboarding, and that is all | Agreement |
| *Organisation cleared* arrives from a product that is offboarding | Ends the terms for the product, *Terms ended*, whenever it arrives, before the notice's end or at it; Organisation runs no clock for a product's notice, which is the product's. If the offboarding was the agreement's and every product's terms have ended, ends the agreement | Agreement |
| *Offboarding requested* with reason declined, for a held agreement | Ends the terms for every product and the agreement at once: an agreement never active has held nothing, so no *Organisation cleared* crosses. Tenancy withdraws the wiring on the events | Agreement |
| An owner's *Offboarding requested* for the installation's agreement is complete under the closing rule: at once under *any owner*; under *every enabled owner* when every enabled owner has requested, one condition over the owners and the request re-evaluated whenever either changes, a request, a withdrawal, *Policy set*, or an owner's role, disabling or deletion, and complete only while no provider's agreement stands | Ends the terms for the control plane and the agreement at once: the control plane holds nothing but the organisation itself, so the notice has nothing to wait for. A request short of complete is pending on the agreement, the other owners told | Agreement |
| *Terms accepted* naming a provider while the installation's dormancy offboarding runs its notice | Lapses the offboarding, *Offboarding lapsed*: its reason no longer holds. The agreement stands as before and the organisation is told. The operator's request for terms expired does not lapse this way | Agreement |
| The notice of the installation's dormancy offboarding ends, not lapsed | Ends the terms for the control plane and the agreement, as on the owners' request. Held as a date on the agreement, so a restart loses nothing | Agreement |
| *Agreement ended*, and no other agreement of the organisation stands | Deletes the users first, *User deleted* for each so Person can drop them, then the organisation, *Organisation deleted* being the account's last message, then their data, keeping only retained records. The last agreement is always the installation's | Organisation |

## An expiry is no sanction

An expiry is one product's: the organisation declined the product's new terms, so it may not take more of the product, and what it holds stays reachable under the terms it was taken under until the provider requests offboarding of the product, with notice, or the owner accepts after all. The product's entry holds it as the terms, beside the product's state, and a reader never derives one from the other. The installation's terms follow the same story; since the control plane has no command class, only offboarding bites, and the new terms story told again for the installation is a hotspot.
