# The control plane

The subdomain every product depends on: what an organisation is to the installation and to a provider. An organisation is created at the installation's front desk and holds an agreement with the installation from that moment, whose product is the control plane; it comes to a provider by accepting one of its products' terms, holds things from its products, gives products back, and leaves, the installation last. It knows the products only at [the seam](../method/seam.md): the terms accepted for each, the quota each is given, each one's answer to offboarding, and what each reports to the catalogue and to the record.

## At a glance

- **Five contexts.** Person, Organisation, Catalogue and Audit on [the map](map/README.md); Tenancy beneath it.
- **Five decisions** every context depends on, and two design notes: [decisions](decisions/README.md).
- **Three contexts designed:** [Organisation](design/organisation/README.md), the root every other context reads from, with its agreement; [Tenancy](design/tenancy/README.md), the tenant, its account and the wiring; [Catalogue](design/catalogue/README.md), the listing every acceptance is validated against.
- **Two standings of one kind of organisation:** those that provide and those that hold. The installation's organisation owns the installation's account and provides the control plane to all; only the account tells it apart. A tenant has no kind.
- **Three principles:** organisations govern themselves and share nothing; records are kept as long as the law requires; an organisation is told of everything concerning what it holds.
- **Drawn from one storming,** as the part that would be the same for any product, and stormed through [nine stories of its own](story/README.md), six from a person's side and three from the provider's, with the example product's facts as their edges. [The method](../method/README.md) says what that means and how a real product tests it.
