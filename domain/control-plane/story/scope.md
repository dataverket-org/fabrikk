# Edges of the story

*Part of [the story](README.md).*

## Left edge: givens

Everything before the left edge is assumed to exist and is not explored. Each given is a candidate for a story of its own.

- The installation's organisation runs the platform: the identity provider, the messaging system, admission, Tenancy, Person, the catalogue and the installation's Audit; its front desk is open. It is a provider, of the control plane: its product is listed in the catalogue under it, with no items and no quota counts, and its terms are published beside it by its operator as any product's are. Every organisation holds an agreement with it from its creation.
- The provider exists, created at the front desk like any organisation, and *Product announced* and *Terms published* have happened: each of its products has been announced as available into the installation's catalogue, with its metadata and the names of its counts, by the service that runs it, which is what made the organisation a provider, and the provider has published each product's terms there beside it. Rules about what an organisation holds are written per product, so that a further product is an addition.
- The room, the power and the network that everything stands on belong to one or more organisations, which may or may not be the installation's. In the installation each is an organisation like any other; what it offers there, and to whom, is the network's own story.
- Whatever a product needs before it can deliver anything is its own given, outside this story.

## Right edge

The story ends when the organisation's data is deleted and, later, when its retained records are. What the provider does with its own assets after an organisation has gone belongs to each product's own story.

## Out of scope

- **The cooperative as a body.** Its membership, governance and foundation are separate stories. It may or may not own a control plane; where it does, it is that installation's organisation, as any organisation that owns one is.
- **Trials, prepaid credits and discounts.** These belong to the money story, not to the story of getting something.
- **Several providers.** An organisation chooses a provider by accepting one of its products' terms, and in this story there is one to choose beside the installation, which provides the control plane to every organisation. The shape holds N, one agreement and one wiring per provider, see [the design note on two lifecycles](../decisions/two-lifecycles.md); a cooperative of several providers, with an organisation holding things from N of them, is a story of its own.
- **How an organisation becomes a provider.** It announces a product into the catalogue; what the installation asks of it first, if anything, is a story of its own.
