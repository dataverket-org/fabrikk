# 4. Leaving

*Part of [the timeline](README.md).* The rules behind each step are [Organisation's commands](../../design/organisation/commands.md) and [policies](../../design/organisation/policies.md); the stories that found them are [an organisation that took nothing closes](../stories/closing.md) and [giving the example product back](../stories/giving-back.md).

1. Offboarding requested *(for one product, or for an agreement and so for every product it holds. By an owner, by choice; or by the provider's operator under the terms, with a reason: declined, dormancy, terms expired or product withdrawn. Every request carries the terms' notice, whoever sent it; the product refuses new asks at once, the editors give back within the notice, and at its end the product ends what is still held. For the installation's agreement it is how an organisation closes, under its policy's closing rule, or how the installation ends a dormant one, with a notice that lapses if a provider's terms are accepted meanwhile)*
2. Organisation cleared, once per product named *(the product's answer as soon as it holds nothing for the organisation, at once if nothing was held; at the notice's end at the latest. None crosses for the control plane, which holds nothing but the organisation itself)*
3. Terms ended, once per product named *(on the answer; the wiring for the product withdrawn. An offboarding of one product ends here, and the agreement stands)*
4. **[P] Agreement ended** *(when the offboarding was the agreement's, once every product's terms have ended; the installation's is always the last)*
5. All its users deleted, then Organisation deleted *(the users first, so Person forgets the organisation from each login; the organisation's deletion is the last fact its account carries)*
6. Organisation data deleted and User data deleted *(apart from records that must be retained)*
7. Person deleted *(when the last validated login tied to the external ID is gone)*
8. Record closed *(Audit holds the organisation's last entry; the retention period starts, and the account is removed)*
9. Retention period ended, then Retained records deleted

Side paths: Offboarding withdrawn *(an owner takes back their own pending request under the every-owner closing rule)*; Offboarding lapsed *(the installation's dormancy offboarding stops of itself when a provider's terms are accepted during its notice)*.
