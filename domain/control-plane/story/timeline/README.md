# The timeline

The control plane's chapters of the board, in order. Events are written as facts in the past tense and use the terms from the [glossary](../../language/README.md). Pivotal events are marked **[P]**. A product's own chapters, where what it delivers is requested, delivered, used and given back, are its own story's; here the example product's facts, as the control plane sees them, stand in chapter 3.

## At a glance

- **Chapters 1, 2 and 4 are sequences.** Chapter 3 is a loop that repeats while an organisation holds things; on a wall its threads run as parallel lanes.
- **Every organisation holds an agreement with the installation,** whose product is the control plane, from its creation to its deletion: born with *Organisation created* in chapter 2, under the terms accepted in the same chapter, and the last to end in chapter 4, since giving it back is closing the organisation.
- **The gates** constrain the order, from the visitor's journey into the organisation's; each names the design page that holds its rule.
- **Audit is the record.** Every command and integration event is an audit entry, kept for the legal period, and an organisation sees every entry about what it holds.
