# 3. While things are held

*Part of [the timeline](README.md).* These threads repeat in any order while an organisation holds things from any product. What a product does to deliver its things is its own story's; the control plane sees only the example product's facts below.

**The example product**
- Resource asked for *(by an editor, an *ask*; accepted against the agreement active, the terms current and the quota, as a reservation, or refused)*
- Resource delivered *(held, named by the product and its own id)*
- Resource given back *(by an editor, a *give back*)*

**Terms**
- New terms published *(by the provider's operator, with an effective date; every organisation that accepted the old ones is told)*, then either Terms accepted again *(by an owner, before the date or after)* or Terms expired *(the product refuses new asks; what is held stays under the old terms; the provider may request offboarding of the product)*

The terms thread runs for the control plane as for any product, told to every organisation; an expiry there refuses no ask, since the control plane has no command class, so only the installation's offboarding for terms expired bites. The new terms story told again for the installation is a hotspot, in [status](../../../open.md).
